What is Agentic ROI (AROI)?
The AROI ratio measures the financial return generated by an agent divided by its complete computational footprint (incorporating physical energy consumed in Joules, carbon offsetting costs, and LLM token API expenses). High AROI indicates a highly optimized, capital-efficient, green agent.
